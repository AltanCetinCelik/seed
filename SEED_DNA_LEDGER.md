## Borrowing rule

Seed may borrow architecture, patterns, small understood snippets, folder design, permission ideas, planning loops, UI ideas, and memory strategies.

Seed must not blindly copy unsafe automation, credential handling, silent system control, or large codebases we do not understand.

Every dangerous action stays approval-gated.
